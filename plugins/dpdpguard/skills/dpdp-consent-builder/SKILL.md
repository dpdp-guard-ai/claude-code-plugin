# DPDPGuard Multilingual Consent & Notice Scaffolder

Generate privacy notices and consent banners compliant with DPDP Act §5 & §6(1), GDPR Art.7, CCPA §1798.120, and PDPA §19.

## Capabilities

- **22 Official Indian Languages**: Full translation dictionary across Eighth Schedule languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, etc.).
- **Opt-In Enforcement**: Guaranteed un-ticked checkboxes (zero assumed consent).
- **Symmetrical Design**: Equal prominence for Accept and Reject buttons (no dark patterns).
- **Notice Generator**: Standalone Privacy Notice route containing Data Fiduciary identity, Purpose list, DSR exercise rights, and Grievance Officer details.
- **Granular Toggles**: Per-purpose consent preferences (Analytics, Marketing, Essential, Personalization).
